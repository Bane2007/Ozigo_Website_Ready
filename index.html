Industrial Insulation

Our LLC - S.P.C | Industrial Division specializes in comprehensive Ozigo General Maintenance solutions tailored for the demanding climates of the Middle East. We provide precision-engineered insulation for piping, tanks, and industrial equipment, ensuring maximum insulation efficiency and operational safety.

Refractory & Cladding

Expert application of high-temperature refractory linings for furnaces, boilers, and incinerators. Our team ensures structural integrity under extreme heat. We also provide professional metal cladding services (Aluminum, SS, GI) to protect insulation systems from mechanical damage and weathering.

Energy Security

In alignment with UAE's sustainability goals, we focus on minimizing energy loss. Our specialized techniques in cryogenic and high-heat insulation help industrial facilities reduce their carbon footprint and optimize energy consumption across all project phases.
